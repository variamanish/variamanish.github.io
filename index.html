01 / Experience

Selected work

My experience spans product engineering, application design, enterprise integration, and production operations. These examples show how I bring that understanding to system modernization and the adoption of AI.

Loan origination

CapitalStream Finance Center

My CapitalStream experience spans product engineering and implementations for banks and credit unions. I’ve designed integrations with credit bureaus and other services, delivered platform enhancements, and supported upgrades and go-lives. That experience informs how I approach the design and evolution of lending systems.

Digital onboarding

Temenos Journey Manager

I designed onboarding workflows, dynamic forms, integrations, and complex services. I helped migrate forms from Composer to Maestro and from Maestro to Open UX, and worked on production performance and security.

Enterprise integration

Fraud data middleware

I architected and implemented middleware to process fraud detection data from Journey Manager, with database persistence and a path to Kafka delivery into a Databricks Lakehouse. The design connects application workflows with the bank’s data architecture.

AI workflow design

Vulnerability remediation

I designed and built an AI workflow that scans an application, reports vulnerabilities, and recommends remediation. Three human approval gates govern the process. Following approval, it upgrades and tests the application, rescans to verify remediation, and produces a final report.

Application modernization

Maestro to Open UX

I designed and built an AI workflow to convert Journey Manager forms from Maestro to Open UX. A feedback loop captures lessons during conversion and reuses them in subsequent runs, allowing the process to evolve with experience.

AI enablement

Skills, MCP, and prototypes

I lead AI enablement planning at Accutive and explore how agentic capabilities fit into existing applications and delivery processes. My hands-on work includes AI skills, Model Context Protocol servers, and prototypes for new Journey Manager form capabilities.

02 / Approach

How I work

I start by understanding the business, the system, and the people who depend on it. That context guides my design decisions.

I look across applications, data, integrations, infrastructure, and security to understand dependencies and make tradeoffs. I evaluate new technology against the problem it needs to solve, the cost of operating it, and how it will evolve. I take ownership of delivery and quality, with attention to commitments, reliability, and the details that matter to the people using the system.

03 / Current focus

What I’m focused on now

I’m designing and developing an AI application to automate software development lifecycle tasks. I’m also architecting an application for loan and lease origination and processing, drawing on my experience with lending platforms and enterprise integration.

As AI evolves, I’m continually learning, experimenting, and assessing where it can deliver value. I combine that adaptability with experience across software systems to design reliable, cost-effective applications, workflows, and processes that can evolve with the business.

I’m drawn to forward-deployed engineering because it connects architecture with the realities of an organization. It means understanding the current environment, shaping a practical solution, and working with the team to carry it through implementation and into operation.
